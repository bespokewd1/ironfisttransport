# Website keyword evidence and data availability

Research date: 2026-11-10. Market: Edmonton, Alberta. Research only.

## Method and limits

Read the November briefs, existing baseline, earlier service-content research, and current local page content before searching. The current user instruction expands deliverable 2 to the main website pages and a planned Moffett article. Measurement setup remains pending client action.

Used the available web search/text-fetch tool. Edmonton and Alberta were query terms. No Edmonton-geolocated Google session, Local Pack capture, personalized results, Search Console export, or rank tracker was used. Returned ordering is not a verified Google ranking. Search snippets can differ from fetched pages and have older content. Dates here mean observation dates, not original publication dates.

Provider pages support terminology and the apparent purpose of their content. They do not establish search frequency, customer conversion rates, organic ranking difficulty, or Iron Fist's capabilities. Proposed exact keywords without an exact matching search are editorial variants within the observed topic, not measured queries.

## Broader website searches actually run

Each query below ran on 2026-11-10. Service-specific searches and their sources are in the three companion evidence memos.

| Exact query | Observation |
| --- | --- |
| `Edmonton construction material transport delivery trucking flatbed Moffett` | Construction freight and forklift-equipped delivery providers appeared. Useful broad discovery, with unrelated results also present. |
| `Edmonton construction industrial transport services owner operator flatbed delivery` | Provider pages appeared alongside employment and unrelated documents. Owner-operated wording describes the business but is not a validated demand term. |
| `"construction material delivery" "Edmonton"` | A dedicated BellSill delivery page appeared, alongside jobs and classifieds. Qualified commercial content is needed. |
| `"transport services" "Edmonton" flatbed Moffett` | Service terminology appeared in broad transport and directory results. The phrase alone does not specify freight or unloading needs. |
| `"Iron Fist Transport" Edmonton` | Brand result and Edmonton Chamber listing appeared. The returned homepage text included old claims and a stale title. Neither proves current deployment state or operating capabilities. |
| `construction material transport Edmonton trucking services -jobs -site:indeed.com -site:kijiji.ca` | Returned building-supplier freight, construction freight and other hauling offers. Aggregate hauling and heavy equipment are adjacent but distinct services. |
| `transportation services Edmonton flatbed power only Moffett -site:ironfisttransport.ca` | Returned multi-service freight providers and directories, with some passenger-transport noise. Prefer service-qualified terms over generic transportation. |

## Local-source facts and editorial consequences

- Homepage and services overview already mention several services. Keep those useful mentions while assigning detailed commercial coverage to the dedicated service pages. Keyword ownership is an editorial preference, not a guarantee of which URL Google will show.
- `/industries/`, `/service-areas/`, `/about/`, `/our-work/` and `/gallery/` exist. Map their supporting roles instead of forcing the same commercial keyword onto every page.
- Current source contains broader Alberta and named-community statements than the October claim-boundary record supports. This research did not locate new owner confirmation. Retain this discrepancy as an implementation fact-check; do not extend it into new city pages or statewide guarantees.
- Existing Moffett content already covers lift weights, access, pickup loading and changed site conditions. A longer copy of that checklist would add little. The recommended article adds a service-choice explanation and a reusable supplier/site enquiry template.
- No present search evidence establishes a November demand spike. Winter can be a brief practical discussion, not the claimed reason for topic selection.
