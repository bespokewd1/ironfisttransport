# Iron Fist Transport keyword research report

2026-11-10 | Edmonton and Alberta | Review draft | Research only

## Recommendation

Use one commercial keyword group for each existing service page. Keep flatbed, flat deck and flatdeck together. Let the homepage introduce the company and the services overview help customers choose. Plan a separate Moffett article about arranging delivery when there is no forklift at the receiving site.

Selections below reflect service relevance and observed search intent. They are not a volume-ranked list. Edmonton is the main local focus. Alberta variants remain conditional on confirmed routes and equipment suitability.

## Why these groups

Local [Bitzer Moffett content](https://www.bitzertrucking.co/pages/services/24-hour-moffett-truck-delivery-services-edmonton) supports a direct commercial Moffett page. [Delivery Tech](https://deliverytech.ca/equipment/flat-decks/) and [Sema](https://sematrucking.ca/) connect flat-deck/flatbed language with freight enquiries. [Oil City Express](https://www.oilcityexpress.com/services) explains truck-and-driver help for a customer's trailer. These sources support the service distinctions, not Iron Fist's operating promises.

[BellSill](https://bellsill.ca/jobsite-construction-material-delivery/) uses construction-material/jobsite delivery language, while [MOVE Logistics](https://www.movelogistics.ca/) groups multiple freight services under a company homepage. This supports broader company and comparison pages alongside detailed service pages. These observations were checked on 2026-11-10 through web search/text fetches, without verified Edmonton geolocation or Google ranking positions.

Avoid actively targeting forklift rental, jobs, load boards, RV towing, equipment sales or unsupported specialist services. Generic trailer transport can attract RV/camper intent. Broad transport terms also need material/freight context. Do not add same-day, 24/7, capacity, price or unrestricted coverage keywords without business evidence.
