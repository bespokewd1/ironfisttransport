## Contextual links for later implementation

1. Homepage service summaries link to the three existing detail pages. Services overview links to those same routes and explains the choice.
2. Industries links to appropriate service pages beside each actual customer situation. About and photo pages link only where the service is relevant.
3. Moffett page links to the proposed article after it exists, using descriptive wording such as `planning delivery when there is no site forklift`.
4. Article links to `/services/moffett-delivery/` for suitability and availability. Link to `/services/flatbed-transportation/` when discussing a load with suitable unloading equipment already arranged.
5. A power-only link belongs beside the customer-supplied-trailer scenario if mentioned, not in an unrelated forklift paragraph.

Repeated natural service mentions across pages are useful. The risk to avoid is publishing several near-identical pages answering the same enquiry, not the mere reuse of a phrase.

## Geographic and intent exclusions

Edmonton is supported as the business base. Research also considered Alberta, but neither competitor pages nor existing marketing text independently verifies every route. Preserve route confirmation language. Candidate `Moffett delivery Alberta`, `flatbed transportation Alberta` and `power only trucking Alberta` can be revisited with owner-confirmed scope and actual query data. Do not create provincial or city landing pages in this phase.

Exclude active targeting of jobs, load boards, forklift rental/sales, trailer rental, passenger transport, RV/camper towing and material shopping. Also exclude unsupported heavy haul, lowboy, step-deck, Super B, crane/picker, hazardous freight, cross-border, emergency and 24/7 promises. Competitor offers do not establish Iron Fist offers.
