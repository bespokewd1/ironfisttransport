## Outline and writing instructions

### Opening: settle the unloading question before arranging the truck

Start with the supplier/site coordination problem in plain language. Explain that a truck-mounted MOFFETT forklift travels with the delivery vehicle and may provide offloading for suitable loads and site conditions. Keep Edmonton context natural. Suitability, route and date need confirmation.

Do not open with a company history, a list of cities or claims that delivery can reach any site.

### H2: What changes when the delivery brings a forklift?

Briefly define MOFFETT as the truck-mounted forklift brand, then use familiar delivery wording. Explain that transporting the load and unloading it are related but separate arrangements.

A vehicle carrying a Moffett can also be a flatbed. Do not frame the choice as mutually exclusive Moffett trucks versus flatbeds. The useful question is whether suitable unloading equipment is already arranged or needs to travel with the delivery.

Describe these two situations in prose:

- Suitable receiving-site equipment and personnel are already arranged. Discuss the load and handling with the carrier; link to flatbed transport where relevant.
- There is no suitable receiving-site equipment. Ask about a delivery carrying its own forklift, with load and access suitability reviewed.

If the reader is unsure, invite a discussion. Do not recommend improvised unloading or claim that every delivery without a site forklift is suitable for this service.

### H2: Check the plan with both the supplier and the receiving site

Explain who is likely to know which information. The supplier can provide packaging, counts, lift weights/dimensions, pickup readiness and available loading equipment. The receiver can provide access details, intended placement, site contact and receiving arrangements.

Ask customers to describe what is available at pickup. Bringing a forklift for offloading does not automatically settle loading at the supplier. Present this as information to discuss, not an invented customer obligation or mandatory booking procedure.

### H2: Copy this information into your delivery enquiry

Make this the article's main practical addition. A plain text template is sufficient; no new form or download is required.

```text
Pickup address:
Supplier contact and pickup hours:
Delivery address:
Requested date and receiving hours:
Receiving-site contact:

Materials and packaging:
Number of pallets, bundles or crates:
Total load weight:
Weight of each lift, with estimates identified:
Length, width and height of each lift:
Loading equipment available at pickup:
Unloading equipment available at delivery, if any:

Where the materials should be placed:
Entrance measurements and overhead restrictions:
Truck area and forklift path:
Ground surface, slopes and soft or uneven areas:
Other work or equipment affecting access:
Photos available:
Any details still to be confirmed:
```

Distinguish total transport weight from each individual lift. Ask for the information available and identify estimates or unknowns. Do not imply that completing the template confirms acceptance or books a date. The current enquiry form may not support attachments; suggest discussing how to share photos rather than promising an upload feature.

### H2: Take photos that explain the route to the drop point

Ask for views of the approach, entrance, truck area, forklift path and intended placement. Explain that the gap between the truck and the storage area matters too. Photos supplement measurements and the carrier's suitability discussion.

Keep this shorter than the template and link to the service page for the existing site details. Do not publish minimum widths, slope limits, reach or lifting capacities without actual equipment and owner confirmation.

### H2: Report changes before the delivery

Briefly cover construction activity, parked equipment, excavations and changed receiving arrangements. Snow, ice or thawed ground can be mentioned as conditions to report. Do not prescribe operating procedures, certify a surface as safe or claim all-weather access. There is no verified Iron Fist winter service policy or measured seasonal demand in this research.

### H2: Discuss your Moffett delivery

Finish with a direct invitation to send the route, date and load/site details to Iron Fist. Link to the real Moffett service page and its enquiry section. A suitable working CTA is: "Have the pickup, load and site details ready? Discuss your Moffett delivery with Iron Fist Transport."
