# Power-only keyword evidence

Research date: 2026-11-10. Market focus: Edmonton, Alberta. Status: research and recommendations only. No site changes or keyword-account datasets.

## Method and limits

Used the available web search tool and text page reads. Edmonton and Alberta were included in queries, but the tool did not provide a verified Edmonton search location or a reproducible Google organic ranking report. Returned sources establish examples of intent and provider wording. Result order, number of results, and directory carrier counts are not keyword demand or organic difficulty metrics.

No Keyword Planner volume export, Search Console query data, or validated keyword difficulty dataset was available to this researcher. Monthly searches, advertiser competition, CPC, organic difficulty, clicks, impressions, and current Iron Fist ranks remain unavailable. Missing values do not mean zero. The parent research report should record any additional tool availability checks separately.

Read the November monthly brief, the existing service research and claim boundaries, and `src/pages/services/power-only-trucking.astro` before researching. The current page already explains tractor and driver versus customer trailer, loaded/empty status, connection and condition details, and handover planning. It needs keyword alignment where justified, rather than another generic definition page.

## Intent and ambiguity

Observed commercial intent includes finding a carrier to provide tractor power for an existing trailer. Anttal, Oil City, Kiara, and J.P. Sidhu supply locally relevant examples. This supports a dedicated service page rather than giving the homepage the primary power-only target.

The generic trailer-transport query also returned [Turbo J RV, boat and light-duty trailer transport](https://turboj.ca/transportation-services/rv-boat-trailer-transport/), [G & D RV Towing](https://www.gdrvtowing.com/), and [Trailer Transport Canada](https://trailertransportcanada.com/). These were search-result observations, not full-page audits. They show that trailer transport alone can attract a different audience. Qualify it with power-only or customer-supplied commercial trailer wording.

The power-only-loads/job query returned recruitment pages, including [Big Boss Carrier's owner-operator page](https://www.bigbosscarrier.com/p/owner-operator-trucking-jobs.html). This was a search extract used only to identify intent ambiguity. Jobs, owner-operator work, load boards, and finding loads are outside this page's enquiry goal.

The semi-trailer-relocation query produced broad nonlocal relocation providers and an Edmonton job posting. It did not establish local demand for that exact phrase. Treat semi-trailer relocation as a possible supporting explanation, not a validated local primary keyword.

## Recommended cluster and page ownership

These priorities are editorial choices based on service fit and observed intent. They do not rank estimated demand or ease of ranking.

| Role | Candidate terms | Recommendation and evidence strength |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | `power only trucking Edmonton` | Strong service fit and relevant local provider evidence. Keep `/services/power-only-trucking/` as owner. Hyphenated power-only is normal editorial spelling; do not create separate pages for punctuation variants. |
| Supporting | `power only tractor service Edmonton`; `tractor service Edmonton` | Local provider language is observed. Use alongside an explicit customer-trailer explanation because tractor service also appears in broader transport contexts. |
| Plain-language support | `tractor and driver for your trailer`; `customer supplied trailer transport Edmonton` | Clear description of the service. The exact phrases are editorial variants supported by the provider explanations, not separately validated query demand. |
| Qualified secondary | `power only trailer transport Edmonton`; `commercial trailer relocation Edmonton` | Useful in relevant body copy if it reads naturally. Relocation demand and exact-term popularity were not established. |
| Industry vocabulary | `hired cartage Edmonton` | Oil City uses this for the relevant customer-trailer scenario, but wider results include labour/contract terminology. Optional body explanation only, rather than a title target. |
| Wider geography | `power only trucking Alberta` | Relevant provincial research seed. Do not imply Alberta-wide acceptance or create an Alberta landing page without confirmed operating scope and distinct useful content. |

The homepage can mention power-only trucking once in its service summary and link to the dedicated page. The services overview should help a customer choose between a customer-supplied trailer move, flatbed transport, and Moffett offloading. It should not repeat the full trailer checklist. Keep the planned November Moffett article outside this cluster except for a short related-service link if useful.

## Terms to exclude from active targeting

- `power only jobs`, `owner operator power only`, `power only loads`, and load-board queries. These primarily risk attracting carriers and job seekers rather than customers requesting a move.
- RV, camper, boat, travel-trailer towing, and light-duty hitch terms. No Iron Fist acceptance evidence supports them.
- Tractor rental, truck rental, or driver-only hire. The confirmed page describes a tractor-and-driver service, not equipment rental or staffing.
- Drop-and-hook programs, dedicated fleet contracts, intermodal drayage, wet-line/PTO, winch-tractor work, hazardous loads, and cross-border routes. Do not turn adjacent competitor services into Iron Fist offerings.
- Cheap, cheapest, same-day, 24/7, fixed per-mile prices, guaranteed pickup, and every-trailer acceptance. These promises have no verified support.

## Proposed future application

The current title, Power-only trucking in Edmonton, already matches the primary cluster. Keep it unless the later implementation review identifies a better reason to change it. The existing opening definition and trailer details answer commercial intent well. Natural supporting wording can fit the introduction and quote guidance without repeating Edmonton in every heading.

Preserve enquiry qualification around trailer condition, connections, loaded or empty status, exact route, release arrangements, dates, suitability, and availability. Any new accepted-trailer list, operating area, rate, turnaround time, or special equipment claim needs owner confirmation first. Do not reuse the old indexed Iron Fist homepage snippet as independent factual evidence.

This memo recommends keyword ownership and content emphasis. It does not authorize implementation, prove search demand, or promise rankings.
